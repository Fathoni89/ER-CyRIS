1.  **Prioritize Supervised Models (XGBoost, RandomForest)**: These models consistently demonstrated superior and robust performance across various scenarios where data was available, including imbalance, shift/drift, and real-time inference simulations. Their accuracy and stability make them suitable for deployment.

2.  **Continuous Monitoring**: Implement robust monitoring for data drift and concept drift in production to detect any changes that might degrade model performance over time, especially considering the observed sensitivity to Gaussian noise for supervised models.

3.  **Investigate Critical Misclassifications (if any appear)**: If any critical False Positives or False Negatives are encountered in future deployments or more diverse test sets, leverage SHAP explanations to understand the contributing features. This can guide targeted feature engineering or data collection efforts.

4.  **Hardware Optimization for Real-time**: Further optimize hardware and inference serving solutions to maximize throughput and minimize latency for real-time anomaly detection, building upon the promising results from the simulation.

5.  **Refine Unsupervised Models (Optional)**: If specific types of novel anomalies are expected that supervised models might miss, consider further research and hyperparameter tuning for unsupervised models (like IsolationForest or LOF) on more representative outlier data. However, based on current results, they require significant improvement before deployment.
